## 🧠 About Me

I'm a **Senior Mobile Engineer, CTO, Solutions Designer and technology researcher** working at the intersection of:

**AI · Mobile Systems · Cloud-Native Architecture · Distributed Intelligence · FinTech · Web3 · Cognitive Systems**

I design and build production-oriented systems across mobile, cloud and intelligent infrastructure, with a particular interest in how **AI agents, distributed systems and human-centric computing** can work together to solve real-world problems.

My work spans from **Flutter/iOS engineering and fintech platforms** to experimental research involving **agentic AI, adaptive cognitive networks, edge intelligence, neural-digital interfaces and decentralized infrastructure**.

Currently focused on building systems that are not only intelligent, but **adaptive, scalable, resilient and useful**.
